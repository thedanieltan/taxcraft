# Complex-composite PIT packages — wave 3

## Scope

Wave 3 adds Norway for calendar year 2026 with three independently reported layers:

- tax on ordinary income;
- bracket tax on personal income;
- salary National Insurance contribution.

The package accepts caller-confirmed tax bases. It does not derive ordinary income, personal income, deductions or allowances.

## Ordinary-income tax

The standard 2026 rate for individuals is 22% of ordinary income after deductions and allowances.

The package requires explicit confirmation that the standard rate applies. The 18.5% rate and special deductions for the Finnmark and Nord-Troms priority zone remain outside scope.

## Bracket tax

The 2026 bracket-tax schedule is:

- no bracket tax through NOK 226,100;
- 1.7% from NOK 226,100 to NOK 318,300;
- 4.0% from NOK 318,300 to NOK 725,050;
- 13.7% from NOK 725,050 to NOK 980,100;
- 16.8% from NOK 980,100 to NOK 1,467,200;
- 17.8% above NOK 1,467,200.

Each marginal band is floored to the nearest Norwegian øre.

## Salary National Insurance contribution

The supported schedule applies to salary income for persons aged 17 to 69:

- standard rate: 7.6%;
- lower limit: NOK 99,650;
- contribution cap during the phase-in: 25% of salary income above the lower limit.

The package calculates the lower of the standard-rate contribution and the 25% phase-in cap. Other age, pension and business-income schedules remain outside scope.

## Deterministic fixture

For NOK 600,000 ordinary-income tax base, NOK 800,000 personal income and NOK 800,000 salary income, the package calculates:

- NOK 132,000.00 ordinary-income tax;
- NOK 28,105.55 bracket tax;
- NOK 60,800.00 National Insurance contribution;
- NOK 220,905.55 total included liability.

## Official sources

- Norwegian Ministry of Finance: adopted 2026 tax rates, allowances and thresholds;
- Norwegian Tax Administration: ordinary-income tax rate;
- Norwegian Tax Administration: bracket-tax rates and thresholds;
- Norwegian Tax Administration: National Insurance contribution rates, lower limit and phase-in cap.

Source observation date: 2026-07-25.

## Exclusions

The package does not calculate or determine:

- gross income, ordinary income, personal income, deductions or allowances;
- personal allowance, minimum standard deduction or other deductions;
- priority-zone rates or deductions;
- pension, business, fishing, hunting, childcare or age-specific contribution schedules;
- wealth tax, dividend gross-up, shareholder tax or employer contributions;
- foreign-worker PAYE, withholding, prepayments or assessment reconciliation;
- residence, source, treaty or social-security coverage.

## Acceptance boundary

Implementation includes the country model, package registry, closed input schema, catalogue and API registration, exact bracket-threshold fixtures, National Insurance phase-in fixtures, privacy tests, official-source attribution and documentation.

Repository acceptance requires the exact branch head to pass the complete repository and hardened-container workflow. Deployment and live acceptance remain separate states.
